# Fair Drop — Threat Model

**Assets**: ticket ownership (500), fairness of allocation, inventory integrity, session integrity, experiment credibility, platform quota.
**Adversary**: an automated client operator with scripting skill, many requests, possibly many IPs (residential proxies), many accounts, a headless browser that can fake behavior, and possibly paid CAPTCHA-solving. Not in scope: Cloudflare compromise, our secrets being stolen.

**Honesty rule**: nothing here is "unexploitable". Each row states what the system actually guarantees and what remains.

| # | Threat | Attack | Intended effect | Counter-mechanism | Residual weakness | Evaluation metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Request flooding | Thousands of `register`/`reserve` calls per second | More chances / exhaust capacity / crowd out humans | L0 edge limit, L1/L2 buckets, semantic dedupe (one entry per account), lottery ignores volume, risk NET_* rules | Distributed floods from many IPs consume Worker/DO request quota (free-tier exhaustion = availability attack). Cloudflare DDoS protection mitigates volumetric attacks, not quota burn on valid-looking requests | `RVA`, `VS(A)`, `rate_limited`, `blocked_edge`, `lottery_entry_violation=0`, P99 latency under flood |
| T2 | Retry storm | Aggressive retries on 429/503/409 | Win by persistence when the system sheds load | Server-dictated `retry_after_ms`, buckets, registration window long enough that shedding only delays, retries collapse to the same participant | Retrying clients get more *latency* priority for status reads (cosmetic only) | `VS` on `retry_amplification`, `deduplicated`, `AAR` |
| T3 | Concurrent requests / race conditions | Parallel `reserve`/`confirm` for the same or different participants, timed at release | Double holds, oversell, two owners for one ticket | Single-threaded DO, `transactionSync`, partial unique indexes, natural-key idempotency, offer capacity invariant | A DO bug would break this (mitigated by continuous invariant check + HALTED). The DO is a single point of throughput (≈ low thousands ops/s) | `oversells`, `duplicate_allocations`, `inventory_drift`, `invalid_reservations` (all 0) under the race scenario |
| T4 | Multiple sessions (same account) | Many browsers/sessions on one account | Multiply entries or holds | Participant keyed by account. Parallel-session risk rule. Account bucket L2 | None for entries. Extra sessions only add load | `duplicate_allocations=0`, `SES_PARALLEL` hits |
| T5 | Duplicate registrations | Re-register, email variants (`a+1@`, `a.b@gmail`) | Multiple entries | Natural key + email normalization + `email_hash` unique | Distinct real mailboxes aren't detected (→ T11) | `deduplicated`, `ineligible_by_reason.DUPLICATE_IDENTITY` |
| T6 | Fake human behavior | Headless browser synthesizing mouse curves, dwell times | Evade risk engine | Multi-category scoring. Network/session rules that are hard to fake cheaply. Turnstile escalation. **Lottery still gives at most 1 entry per account even if detection is fully evaded** | A good human-like bot on one account is indistinguishable from a human and gets exactly the human's odds. That is the designed worst case, not a failure | `BAR` vs `HAR` for HUMAN_LIKE_BOT (expected ≈ equal in FAIR), detection recall (model-dependent) |
| T7 | Low-and-slow bots | Stay below all rate limits, behave plausibly | Avoid detection | Volume and speed confer nothing in the lottery, so slowness gains no advantage | Not detected. Gets equal odds per account | `BAR(SLOW_BOT) ≈ HAR`. No advantage expected or claimed |
| T8 | Refresh/reconnect abuse | Mass refresh, reconnect to reset state or re-roll | Reset a losing/expired state, get a new offer | State in DO keyed by account. Terminal states are final. NAV_REFRESH / SES_RECONNECT rules | Refresh storms cost requests (quota) | Refresh scenario: participant status unchanged, `SES_RECONNECT` hits |
| T9 | Session/token reuse | Copy session cookie to other devices/workers, replay Turnstile tokens | Share one eligible identity or bypass challenge | HMAC cookie bound to `device_id`. Cross-device reuse → HARD_TOKEN_REUSE → revoke + RESTRICT. Turnstile tokens single-use, checked against `cdata=participant_id` | A cookie copied *with* its device_id (same localStorage) can't be distinguished. It still maps to one participant, so there's no gain in entries | `token_reuse` detections, `challenge_failed` |
| T10 | Hold squatting | Winners (or bots) hold tickets and never confirm, to deny others | Waste inventory | Short hold TTL, offer TTL, expiry → AVAILABLE + waitlist promotion, one hold per participant | Inventory may go unsold if the waitlist is exhausted or the booking window ends | `expired_holds`, `promoted`, unsold at close |
| T11 | Sybil / multi-account | Operator registers K accounts with distinct emails, devices, IPs | K lottery entries | Email normalization, device/network clustering signals, per-network registration risk, Turnstile per suspicious account | **Not solvable without strong identity** (verified phone, government ID, payment instrument, loyalty history). An operator with K independent-looking accounts gets ≈ K× the odds. FAIR still removes *volume/speed* advantage, so cost scales with accounts, not requests | `OWR`, tickets per operator, `SES_SHARED_DEVICE` hits. MULTI_SESSION_BOT scenario reports per-operator outcome openly |
| T12 | Seed manipulation by operator | Insider picks a seed or edits the pool after seeing results | Rig the lottery | Commit-reveal: commitment before registration, snapshot hash fixed at freeze, final seed bound to snapshot. Public audit recompute | The server knows `server_seed` before close and could *grind* inserted fake participants until a preferred outcome results. Mitigation options: public randomness beacon (drand) mixed in after freeze (OD-4) | Audit verify: commitment, snapshot, result hash |
| T13 | Sim/admin API abuse | Call `/sim/*` publicly, spoof `X-FD-Sim-Client` to dodge IP limits | Bypass controls, poison results | Admin cookie for creation. Scoped, expiring HMAC sim tokens. Virtual network header honored only on `kind=EXPERIMENT` drops. Experiment drops are separate DOs from public drops | Leaked admin passphrase = full sim control (no impact on the public drop's inventory) | Negative auth test suite |
| T14 | Telemetry poisoning | Send forged "human" telemetry summaries | Lower risk score | Telemetry is one category capped at 35. Network/session features are server-observed. Telemetry can lower risk only by withholding negative interaction points, never subtract network points | A full forgery reduces the score by at most the interaction contribution | Ablation: risk with/without client telemetry |
| T15 | Quota exhaustion (platform) | Push daily Worker/DO/D1 quotas to their limit | Take the demo down | Edge limits, batching, aggregated writes. Can move to Paid | On the Free tier this is a real availability risk | `budget.*` per run, daily quota dashboard |

## Guarantees vs non-guarantees

**Guaranteed by construction (assuming correct implementation):** I-1…I-7 and I-12. Specifically: no oversell, no double ownership, one entry per account, volume and speed don't enter allocation, idempotent retries, refresh-safe state, verifiable lottery given the commitment.

**Probabilistic / best-effort:** behavioral detection, Turnstile effectiveness, cross-account clustering.

**Not provided:** strong Sybil resistance, resistance to an operator who controls the server before freeze (partially mitigated by OD-4), availability against quota-exhaustion on the Free tier.
